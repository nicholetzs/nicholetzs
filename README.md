## ✨ About Me

I'm a **Data Scientist** and **Developer** focused on applying Machine Learning and data pipelines to real problems, especially in **Astronomy**.

My main interests are the analysis of **light curves**, exoplanet transit signals and the development of tools that help turn heterogeneous astronomical data into reliable information.

Currently, I am developing **Orbitalia** — my undergraduate thesis — a collaborative platform for standardization and automated classification of light curves observed by amateur astronomers.

I also have experience with geospatial data, APIs and full-stack development from previous work.

## 🚀 Current Project — Orbitalia

**Orbitalia** *(TCC - 2026)*  
Collaborative platform for preprocessing and automated classification of light curves from amateur astronomers.

- Pipeline for ingestion, cleaning and standardization of photometric data  
- Machine Learning models to classify transit signals vs false positives  
- Evaluation of the impact of data standardization on classification performance  
- Support for citizen science observations in exoplanet research
